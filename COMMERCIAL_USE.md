## Detailed Categories

### ✅ NON-COMMERCIAL (Allowed Without Permission)

#### 1. Personal Use
- Learning and experimentation
- Personal projects and hobby development
- Side projects not generating revenue

#### 2. Open-Source Software
- Using in GPL, MIT, Apache 2.0, or other open-source licensed projects
- Must comply with original license requirements
- Must maintain attribution and license notices

#### 3. Internal Business Use
- Building internal tools for your organization
- Automating your team's workflows
- Internal process improvement
- **Key requirement**: Not offered, sold, or provided to external customers

#### 4. Educational Use
- University coursework and research
- Technical training and bootcamps
- Educational institutions and non-profits
- Classroom demonstrations

#### 5. Non-Profit Use
- Charitable organizations
- Community projects
- Educational research

### ❌ COMMERCIAL (Requires License Agreement)

#### 1. Direct Sales
- Selling this Software (as-is or modified)
- Licensing this Software to customers
- Including in commercial software bundles
- Offering as a commercial product

#### 2. Commercial Services
- Hosting this Software as a service (SaaS)
- Offering consulting services based on this Software
- Providing integration or deployment services for a fee
- Support contracts or maintenance services
- Training services where the Software is a core component

#### 3. Derivative Commercial Products
- Creating a commercial tool that uses this Software
- Building a commercial platform on top of this Software
- Selling modified versions of this Software
- Creating commercial add-ons or plugins

#### 4. Commercial Integration
- Integrating this Software into client projects for resale
- Using as part of commercial deliverables to clients
- Bundling with other commercial products
- Including in commercial applications for resale

#### 5. Commercial Distribution
- Distributing this Software through commercial channels
- Selling licenses or subscriptions
- Offering as part of a commercial offering
- Including in a commercial package deal

## Obtaining a Commercial License

If your use case requires a Commercial License Agreement:

### Step 1: Assess Your Use Case
Review the scenarios above and this document to confirm you need a commercial license.

### Step 2: Contact mgtinker
Create a GitHub Issue in the [mgtinker/Tools](https://github.com/mgtinker/Tools) repository:
- **Title**: "Commercial License Inquiry"
- **Body**: Describe your intended use case
  - What is the commercial product or service?
  - How will this Software be used?
  - Expected revenue or customer base?
  - Timeline for launch?

### Step 3: License Agreement
mgtinker will work with you to:
- Confirm your use case requires a license
- Discuss terms and pricing
- Create a Commercial License Agreement
- Grant you commercial use rights
